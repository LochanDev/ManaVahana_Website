Privacy & Security

Your Data Stays Yours.

ManaVahana follows an offline-first approach so your core vehicle records remain on your device.

📱

100% Offline

Core vehicle records are designed to work without an internet connection. Your data lives on your device — not on a remote server.

🚫

No Cloud Tracking

The product is designed around keeping vehicle information private. No background sync, no usage analytics, no data collection.

🔐

AES-256 Encrypted Backup

Create password-protected, encrypted backups of your complete vehicle data. Restore on any device when needed.

👆

Biometric Protection

Use Android fingerprint or face authentication to protect access to your document vault and sensitive vehicle records.
